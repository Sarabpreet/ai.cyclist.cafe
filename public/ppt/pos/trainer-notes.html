SLIDE 1 · Start here · All staff

The café day,
done properly

Use this deck for induction and shift refreshers. The required staff operating procedure is Open → Use system → Hand over → Close day. Demonstrate actions using training records only; do not submit test payments, stock changes, payroll or messages into the live café. All amounts and staff examples in this deck are fictional. The deck describes the current local implementation as of 31 October 2026; verify the release before training a different deployment.

SLIDE 4 · Start here · All staff

Your login and the drawer are separate

Ask trainees to identify their name in the header and the cashier shown on Home. These can be different. Never share a password to impersonate the cashier. Other authorized staff can build orders, send KOTs and take non-cash payments; cash payments, drawer movements and expenses are restricted to the assigned cashier. Administration has explicit takeover / close override routes with recorded reasons.

SLIDE 19 · Orders & payments · All staff

Use the right POS bucket

Paid bills leave the POS Active bucket, even when preparation continues in Kitchen. Drafts are deliberately behind three consecutive taps on Active. Tapping another bucket resets that gesture. A draft is not a paid bill and does not reserve stock. Reopen saved work instead of recreating it.

Advance tickets when the work actually happens

Marking Preparing triggers consumption of mapped recipe reservations; an inaccurate stage can distort expected ingredient usage.

SLIDE 27 · Kitchen & floor · All staff

Payment and preparation have different jobs

Payment removes a bill from POS Active because it no longer needs collection. It must not hide a meal that still needs preparing or delivery. Conversely, a delivered order can still need payment. Use the appropriate module rather than altering the other state to remove an entry.

Revenue, unpaid estimates and sold-on-credit are different figures. Do not treat pipeline as collected revenue or a sales figure as profit. A printed-bill record is evidence of the print workflow, not a guarantee that paper physically emerged. KOT-to-invoice turnaround and kitchen preparation duration measure different intervals.

Glossary

POS
Point of sale: order entry, checkout and receipts.
KOT
Kitchen Order Ticket: the preparation instruction for a ticket / round.
CRM / People
The shared customer profile used for purchases, loyalty, notes and event participation.
Opening float
Cash physically in the drawer when the register opens; not sales.
Cash difference / variance
Actual physical cash minus expected drawer cash.
X-report
An interim register snapshot while the drawer stays open.
Z-report
The final closing record after cash reconciliation.
Reserved ingredients
Mapped quantities held for dispatched orders before preparation.
Available ingredients
On-hand quantity less reserved quantity.
Recovery
Saved local operations / conflicts and evidence that must be reviewed or synced safely.
