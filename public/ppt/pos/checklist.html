STAFF SHIFT SOP · 31 OCTOBER 2026

The daily counter checklist

Open → Use system → Hand over → Close day

01

OPEN

  • Sign in with your own account. Read the cashier status on Home.
  • Sync saved work on every terminal. Check tasks, roster, printer and low stock.
  • Count the opening float. Choose Open register, enter the physical count and acknowledge responsibility.
02

USE SYSTEM

  • Confirm order type, table if dine-in, customer, quantities and drink options.
  • Send unsent items to Kitchen. Advance kitchen stages only when the work happens.
  • Verify actual payment. Cash and split cash are handled by the assigned cashier while connected.
  • Record cash expenses, safe drops and added float once, with an accurate reason.
03

HAND OVER

  • Sync all terminals and explain open bills, kitchen follow-ups and unresolved issues.
  • Count the drawer. Request handover to an eligible staff member and explain any cash difference.
  • Pause cash handling. Incoming cashier signs in as themselves, checks cash and accepts.
  • Confirm the new cashier name. Signing out alone never transfers responsibility.
04

CLOSE DAY

  • Review unpaid bills / credit and unfinished service with the person in charge.
  • Record all real movements, sync every terminal and count the final physical cash.
  • Choose Close day. Review expected cash, enter the actual count and explain any difference.
  • Wait for confirmed closure. Use the saved Z-report / archive for the closing record.

Uncertain save or payment? Review the existing ticket / Sync → Recovery. Do not collect twice, recreate the sale or clear browser storage.

Expected cash = opening float + cash sales + added float − safe drops − cash expenses.
Cash operations need a connection. Follow the café’s separate cleaning, food-safety, key and safe-custody procedures.
